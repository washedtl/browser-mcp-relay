Cookie bridge

Review which browser logins get copied into the relay's broker browser and kept in sync. Nothing leaves this browser until you approve. Banking, email, and other sensitive sites are blocked by default, and cookie values are never shown here or sent to the model.

Toggles take effect immediately once sync is approved. Un-ticking a domain tells the broker to drop its cookies; newly seen keyword-flagged domains appear under "Held for review" with a badge on the extension icon until you decide.
